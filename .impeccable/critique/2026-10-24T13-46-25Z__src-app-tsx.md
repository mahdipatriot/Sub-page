## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Desktop hero wastes ~40% of its surface** — in the default active state the Connect pane (two buttons + one link) leaves a large blank region beside the status pane (visible at 1280px in light and dark); the page's face reads as unfinished and contradicts bold/fast/premium. Fix: anchor the space — featured-app card or QR preview in the Connect pane, or a full-width status band with a compact action row; minimum: vertically center the cluster and place the signal motif at low opacity in the void. Suggested command: impeccable layout.
2. **[P1] Silent infinite retry makes degradation invisible** — useUserInfo retries every 5s forever; with stale data the page never signals reconnection, and the error card hides the ongoing loop (isValidating gating). Users on flaky networks can't tell frozen from busy. Fix: surface retry state in the به‌روزشده aria-live line ("در حال تلاش مجدد…") and add "دوباره تلاش می‌کنیم" to the error card. Suggested command: impeccable harden.
3. **[P2] The featured app disappears exactly when needed** — AppsList removes the featured app from its platform group; with one app per platform the user's own OS chip vanishes (observed live: Windows showed only iOS/Android chips). Fix: pin the featured app's card with a "برای این دستگاه" marker instead of removing it, or always show a one-line featured strip even when steps are collapsed. Suggested command: impeccable clarify.
4. **[P2] Copy/typography tells in the most-read lines** — freshWhen always uses plural ("Updated: 1 mins ago"); stepPaste embeds a parenthetical Latin gloss inside Persian text; line-clamped RTL announcements can clip at the wrong visual end. Fix: singular time.min key with count logic; restyle the gloss as a bdi chip or drop it; dir="auto" on the clamping element. Suggested command: impeccable typeset.
5. **[P3] Three selection idioms for the same interaction** — platform chips (black, aria-pressed), time-range chips (purple), dropdown radios. Fix: one segmented-chip component with one selected style reused in AppsList and TrafficChart. Suggested command: impeccable polish.
